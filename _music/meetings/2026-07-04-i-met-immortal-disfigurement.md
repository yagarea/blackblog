Long time ago when I was in high school and was getting into more extreme music
I discovered video with about 500 views called [SIGNS OF THE SWARM - CESSPOOL OF IGNORANCE [OFFICIAL MUSIC VIDEO] (2017) SW EXCLUSIVE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xFRmvS3EaVc).
I still remember I got goose bumps when I heard this song for the first time.
It was the most insane vocal I have ever heard. I could not believe this was possible for human to make sounds like that.

Thats why it was such a huge deal for me to finally meet the artist himself.
